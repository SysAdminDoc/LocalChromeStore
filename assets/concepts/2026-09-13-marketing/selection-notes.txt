LocalChromeStore identity review, 2026-09-13

Selected: concept 02, app icon.

Why it won:
- The storefront and extension-piece silhouette explain the product without using Chrome's trademarked browser mark.
- The dark tile matches the Windows app and keeps its shape on light or dark surfaces.
- Broad color blocks stay recognizable at taskbar size.
- The geometry feels deliberate and avoids the soft, generic 3D treatment in the original asset.

Concept 01 was clear but looked more like a generic marketplace. Concept 03 remains useful as a wordmark study. Concept 04 felt too much like a badge. Concept 05 was compact but lost the store idea.

The original logo, original banner, all five directions, the rendered comparison sheet, and the selected production sources are retained in this folder or the adjacent brand folder.
